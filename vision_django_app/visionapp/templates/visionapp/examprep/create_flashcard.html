Tips for Great Flashcards

  • Keep questions specific - Ask one thing at a time
  • Use your own words - This helps with understanding
  • Include context - Add examples when helpful
  • Be concise - Shorter cards are easier to review
  • Test understanding - Ask "why" and "how" questions

Example Flashcard

Q: What is the time complexity of binary search?
A: O(log n) - Binary search divides the search space in half with each step.
